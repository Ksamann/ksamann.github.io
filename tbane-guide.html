Metro timing logger
User guide

Record the real running time between stations and the dwell time at each station on Oslo T-bane lines 1–5, using taps on your phone. Results can be exported as CSV or JSON and shared by email.

1Opening the app

  • Open ksamann.github.io/tbane.html in your phone's browser.
  • To open it like an app, add it to your home screen: in Safari tap Share → Add to Home Screen; in Chrome tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen.
  • The app is a single file. It also works when opened from SharePoint or saved on the phone, and it needs no connection while you log.
  • Switch language with NO EN at the top. Station names always stay in Norwegian.

2Setting up a trip

  1. Line: tap the coloured circle for line 1–5.
  2. Direction: tap the terminus you are travelling towards.
  3. Section: the whole line is shown as a diagram. With Pick start selected, tap the station where you board. The app then switches to Pick end; tap the station where you will stop logging. Stations outside the section are greyed out.
  4. Tap Next → at the bottom.

Line 5 passes through the city centre twice and around the Ring line, so some stations appear twice in the list. Pick the one at the right place in the sequence.

Diversions or errors in the station list? Tap ✎ Edit line under the diagram to rename, remove or insert stations, then Save line. Changes are stored on your phone only. Reset restores the default list.

3Trip details

  • Observer (required): your name. It is remembered for the next trip.
  • Train / unit (optional): train number or unit number.
  • Note (optional): anything about the trip, such as rush hour or delays.

Tap Start logging. It stays disabled until a name is entered.

4Logging

The large button alternates between two states. Tap it at the exact moment the train moves or stops:

  1. DEPART: tap when the train starts moving from a station.
  2. ARRIVE: tap when the train has come to a stop at the next station.
  3. Repeat for each station.
  • The large timer shows the time since your last tap: dwell time while standing at a station, run time while moving.
  • The list below the button shows logged stations with the newest at the top: arrival time, dwell time, and the run time between stations. The control panel stays in place when you scroll the list.
  • ↶ Undo removes your last tap. Use it if you tapped too early or by mistake.
  • ✎ Note adds a note to the current station, for example door blocked or held at signal.
  • A second tap within 0.4 seconds is ignored, so an accidental double tap does not register twice.
  • The screen is kept awake while logging, and the phone vibrates briefly on each tap where supported.

Every tap is saved immediately. If the browser closes or the phone restarts, open the app again and logging continues where you left off.

5Finishing a trip

  • The trip finishes automatically when you tap ARRIVE at the end station you picked.
  • To stop earlier, tap Finish at the top and confirm with Finish trip.
  • The summary shows total time, total running time, total dwell time and a table per station.
  • If the trip ended by mistake, tap ↶ Resume trip on the summary to continue logging.

6Exporting and sharing

  • Share (email, Teams …) opens the phone's share menu with both the CSV and the JSON file attached. Pick Mail, Outlook, Teams or similar.
  • CSV or JSON downloads the file to the phone.
  • If sharing isn't supported in the browser, both files are downloaded instead.

The CSV has one row per station:

ColumnContent
trip_id, date, line, directionIdentifies the trip. Direction is the terminus.
seq, stationOrder and station name.
arrival, departureTimestamps with milliseconds and time zone, e.g. 2026-11-23T17:27:31.123+01:00.
dwell_sSeconds at the station (departure − arrival).
to_station, run_sNext station and seconds from departure here to arrival there.
station_note, observer, train, trip_noteYour notes and trip details.

CSV format follows the app language. In English: comma separator and decimal point. In Norwegian: semicolon separator, decimal comma and Norwegian column names, which opens correctly in Norwegian Excel. Switch language before exporting if the recipient needs the other format. The JSON file always uses English keys and also contains every individual tap.

7History

  • Tap History on the setup screen to see all trips stored on the phone.
  • Tap a trip to see its summary and export it again.
  • Share sends one trip; Delete removes it after you confirm.
  • Export all (CSV) or Export all (JSON) exports every trip in one file.

8Tips and troubleshooting

  • Data lives only on this phone. Export or share trips before clearing browser data or switching phones. Private/incognito tabs don't keep data.
  • Tap on the movement, not the doors. Use the same rule on every trip, such as first wheel movement and full stop, so the numbers are comparable.
  • Screen turned off anyway? Some phones ignore the keep-awake request in power-saving mode. Turn power saving off or increase the auto-lock time.
  • Missed a tap? Tap as soon as you can and add a ✎ Note so the value can be corrected later.
  • Wrong station or direction? Finish the trip, delete it in History, and start again.
Station lists are based on the current T-bane service pattern (lines 1–5) and can be edited in the app. Temporary diversions are not included.
