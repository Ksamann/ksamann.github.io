Banepuls
User guide

Banepuls shows the Oslo metro in realtime and lets you time trains yourself. Every time study is linked to the actual departure in Entur's open data, so you see three times for each stop: the plan, the realtime data, and what you measured.

1Opening the app

  • Open ksamann.github.io/banepuls. Install it from the browser menu (Share → Add to Home Screen in Safari, ⋮ → Install app in Chrome).
  • After the first visit the app opens without signal. Realtime needs a connection; logging does not.
  • Switch language under More. Station names stay in Norwegian.
  • The original apps, Linjeblikk and the T-bane logger, still work. Trips from the T-bane logger can be imported (see Trips).

2The Now tab

  • Station: departures from your station, grouped by direction, with where each train is now and a “leave in” countdown that uses your walking time. The nearest station is found from your position if you allow it; you can also pick one.
  • Each departure has Log (start a time study on that train) and Ping (see Ping).
  • Line: every train on one line, both directions. Tap a train for details and Log from ….
  • Map: the whole network with trains in realtime. Drag, pinch or use the buttons to zoom.
  • Timetable: a graphic timetable of the common tunnel or Tøyen–Brynseng. Steep lines are running trains, vertical lines are trains standing still.
  • Service messages from Ruter (for example rail replacement buses) appear on the station view.

3Time study

  1. Tap Log on a departure, or go to Log, choose Time study and pick the train.
  2. The app fetches the whole departure. Choose where to stop logging under End at, and add a note if you like.
  3. The clock is synced with the server before you start. The line under the button tells you how far off your phone was; this is corrected in the results.
  4. Tap Start time study. Then tap the large button the moment the train starts moving (DEPART) and the moment it has stopped (ARRIVE), station by station. The time is taken when your finger touches the screen.
  • While logging, the table under the button shows Plan, Realtime and Measured for each tap, with Δ = measured minus realtime. The panel also shows the realtime time the system expects for your next tap.
  • ↶ Undo removes the last tap; ✎ Note adds a note to the current station.
  • The trip ends by itself when you arrive at the end station, or with Finish. If it ended by mistake, use ↶ Resume trip on the result.
  • No signal or no realtime? Choose Set up by hand to pick line, direction, start and end yourself, as in the T-bane logger. Plan and realtime are then missing.

4Reading the result

  • Mean deviation and largest deviation compare your taps with Entur's realtime times. A positive Δ means the event happened later than realtime said.
  • Used vs plan compares the time from your first departure to your last arrival with the timetable.
  • The chart draws plan (dashed), realtime (blue) and your measurements (white) on the same time axis.
  • Realtime shown in grey-blue is a forecast; full colour is an actual time reported by the system. Times marked ≈ are identical to the plan on the whole minute and were probably copied from it, so they are left out of the comparison.

Why the app fetches realtime straight away. Entur keeps actual times for the metro for only about an hour. Banepuls fetches them during the trip and a few times right after. Keep the app open for a minute after the trip if you can.

What realtime doesn't have. For the metro, realtime usually has a single time per stop, with arrival equal to departure. Dwell times therefore come only from your taps, which makes them especially valuable.

5Platform mode

  • Stand on one station and log every train that passes. Go to Log → Platform mode → start.
  • There is one big button per direction. Tap ARRIVE when a train stops and DEPART when it starts moving. Each train is matched to its departure in realtime.
  • The result shows dwell time per train and the headway between trains in the same direction, with medians.

6Ping

  • A one-tap record that you were on a particular train, with plan and realtime at that moment. No timing.
  • Afterwards you can say how it went: On time, Delayed or Crowded.

7Sharing a train

  • Tap ↗ during a time study, on a train's details, or on a result to share a link to that train.
  • Whoever opens the link sees where the train is now, the next stops and, during a time study, when it arrives at your end station. No account or app needed.
  • Trains can only be followed on the day they run.

8Sensor assistance (beta)

  • Turn it on under More → Sensor assistance. On iPhone you are asked to allow motion access when you start a time study.
  • The accelerometer notices when the train starts moving and when it has stopped. It then shows a suggestion under the button, for example Sensor: departure 12:03:14. Tap Use to log that time, or just tap the big button yourself.
  • Your own taps are always the reference. Sensor detections are stored separately, and the result shows how closely they matched your taps.
  • Keep the phone still (in your hand or on your lap) for best results. Walking or handling the phone is ignored.
  • Save raw sensor data stores a compact signal with the trip for calibration. It makes the JSON file larger.

9Trips and trends

  • All time studies, platform sessions and pings are stored on the phone, newest first. Tap one to see its result again.
  • My trends shows the median running time on the legs you have measured most, against the timetable, and how far realtime has been from your measurements on average.
  • Import reads JSON files from Banepuls or the T-bane logger, for example to move data to a new phone.

10Sending data for analysis

  • Data never leaves the phone unless you send or share it. There is no account and no tracking.
  • To send to someone who collects data, add their address under More → Analysis address, or open the invitation link you were given (it fills in the address).
  • Send for analysis opens your mail app with a short summary and the data, ready to send. Very long sets are shared as files instead and the address is copied for you.
  • Your name and who you are (for example Sporveien, enthusiast) are optional and only included if you fill them in.
  • Share, CSV and JSON give you the files directly.

11Analysis tool

  • For whoever receives data: open the analysis tool (also under More).
  • Add Banepuls or T-bane logger JSON files, saved emails (.eml, .txt), or paste the text of emails sent with Send for analysis. Many at once is fine; duplicates are skipped.
  • Views: Overview, Running times per leg against the timetable, Dwell times per station, Realtime accuracy per station, Double measurements (the same departure timed by two people) and Headways from platform mode.
  • Filter by line, time band, role and dates, and download the table or all data as CSV.
  • Nothing is uploaded. The combined data is kept in this browser until you press Clear.

12Data formats

The CSV has one row per measured stop (time study), per train (platform) or per ping. The most important columns:

ColumnContent
kind, journey_idstudy, platform or ping, and the departure's ID in Entur
plan_arrival, plan_departureTimetable times
rt_arrival, rt_departure, rt_is_actual, rt_equals_planRealtime, whether it was an actual time, and whether it equalled the plan
measured_arrival, measured_departureYour taps, corrected with the clock offset, with milliseconds and time zone
delta_arrival_s, delta_departure_sMeasured minus realtime, in seconds
dwell_s, run_s, headway_sDwell, running time to the next stop, and headway (platform mode)
arrival_source, departure_sourcemanual, or sensor when a sensor suggestion was used
clock_offset_msThe correction applied: server time minus phone time

The JSON file (version 3) has the raw taps with the phone's own clock, the realtime snapshot, the clock sync and all derived values. In English the CSV uses commas and decimal points; in Norwegian semicolons and decimal commas.

13Tips

  • Use the same rule every time: first wheel movement for departure, full stop for arrival.
  • Underground with no signal you can keep logging; realtime is fetched when the connection returns, as long as it is within the hour.
  • Screen turned off? The app keeps the screen on while logging, but power-saving modes can override it.
  • Data lives only on this phone. Send or export before clearing browser data or changing phones.
Realtime and timetable data: Entur (open data, NLOD). Banepuls is an independent app and not made by Ruter or Sporveien.
